## Theme Analysis & Recommendation

### Mintaka Theme Analysis
**Strengths:**
- ✅ **Perfect structure match** - Hero, Services, Work Preview, FAQ sections align perfectly
- ✅ **Blog capability** - Built-in blog system with categories and authors
- ✅ **Project showcase** - Dedicated project/portfolio section
- ✅ **Professional design** - Modern, clean, business-focused
- ✅ **Multi-language support** - Good for international clients
- ✅ **Content-rich** - Can handle extensive testimonials and detailed service descriptions

**Content Fit:**
- ✅ **Hero section** - Rotating text feature perfect for key messages
- ✅ **Work Preview** - Can showcase companies we've worked with
- ✅ **Services** - Multiple service descriptions with detailed explanations
- ✅ **Blog** - 5 proposed articles fit perfectly
- ✅ **Contact** - Professional contact section

### DatoCMS Theme Analysis
**Strengths:**
- ✅ **Monorepo structure** - More complex, enterprise-focused
- ✅ **Component library** - Reusable UI components
- ✅ **Headless CMS** - More flexible content management

**Limitations:**
- ❌ **Complexity overkill** - More complex than needed for current content
- ❌ **CMS dependency** - Requires DatoCMS setup and maintenance
- ❌ **Less content-focused** - More suited for larger, multi-brand operations

## Final Recommendation: **Mintaka Theme** 

### Why Mintaka is Perfect for Toki Labs:

1. **Content Volume Match** - We have rich, detailed content that needs proper showcase
2. **Business Focus** - Professional design that emphasizes expertise and results
3. **Blog Capability** - Built-in blog system for thought leadership content
4. **Project Showcase** - Perfect for "Companies We've Worked With" section
5. **Testimonial Support** - Can handle multiple detailed testimonials effectively
6. **International Appeal** - Multi-language support for global partnerships
7. **Modern Design** - Aligns with "works differently" positioning
