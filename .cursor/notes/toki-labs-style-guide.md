# Toki Labs Writing Style Guide

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Core Personality
- **Approachable Expert** - Technical depth without intimidation
- **Human-Centered** - Technology serves people, not the other way around
- **Results-Driven** - Every story connects to measurable impact
- **Authentic** - Genuine passion for positive change

### Tone Characteristics
- **Conversational yet Professional** - "Hey there!" energy with business substance
- **Confident but Humble** - Share achievements without arrogance
- **Optimistic** - Focus on possibilities and positive outcomes
- **Empathetic** - Understand client challenges and human needs

## Writing Principles

### 1. Lead with Impact
- Start with the business or human benefit
- Use specific metrics and percentages when possible
- Connect technical solutions to real-world outcomes
- Example: "Reduced design system creation time by 70%" not just "Created a design tool"

### 2. Tell Stories, Don't Just List Features
- Use narrative structure: Challenge → Solution → Result
- Include context about why the work mattered
- Mention the human element (team size, time constraints, stakes)
- Example: "Coordinated a 40-person team to deliver critical changes in one month when Facebook removed the Instagram API"

### 3. Make Complex Simple
- Explain technical concepts in business terms
- Use analogies when helpful
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- When using technical terms, briefly explain their business value

### 4. Show, Don't Just Tell
- Use concrete examples over abstract descriptions
- Include client names when possible (with permission)
- Mention specific technologies and their benefits
- Quantify achievements wherever possible

### 5. Write Like a Human, Not a Marketing Bot
- Vary sentence and paragraph lengths naturally
- Include conversational asides and genuine thoughts
- Admit when something was harder than expected or didn't go perfectly
- Use contractions and casual language where appropriate
- Share the messy, imperfect reality behind the polished outcomes
- Start with stories and personal experiences, not abstract concepts

## Content Structure Templates

### Project/Achievement Description
```
**Challenge:** [What problem needed solving]
**Approach:** [How we tackled it, including any innovations]
**Impact:** [Measurable results and business value]
**Technologies:** [Brief mention of key tools used]
```

### Client Testimonial Integration
- Always include the person's role and company
- Choose quotes that emphasize business impact, not just technical skill
- Use testimonials that show different aspects of our expertise

### Service Description
```
**What We Do:** [Clear, jargon-free explanation]
**Why It Matters:** [Business benefit to the client]
**Our Approach:** [What makes us different]
**Typical Results:** [Expected outcomes with examples]
```

## Language Guidelines

### Preferred Terms
- "Partners" instead of "clients" (aligns with company philosophy)
- "Transform" instead of "change" or "improve"
- "Innovation" for new approaches or technologies
- "Optimize" for efficiency improvements
- "Architect" for complex system design

### Avoid
- Overly technical jargon without context
- Superlatives without backing (avoid "best," "perfect," "revolutionary")
- Passive voice when active is clearer
- Industry buzzwords without substance

### AI-Sounding Language to Avoid
- "In today's rapidly evolving [industry]..."
- "Let's dive deep into..."
- "Key takeaways include..."
- "Cutting-edge solutions"
- "Game-changing innovation"
- Perfectly structured sections with identical formatting
- Generic opening paragraphs that could apply to any company
- Lists of benefits without specific examples or context

## Messaging Hierarchy

### Primary Messages
1. **Partnership Approach** - "We seek and support partners, not just clients"
2. **Complex Problem Solving** - "We thrive in complexity where others see chaos"
3. **Measurable Impact** - "Every solution delivers quantifiable business value"
4. **Social Responsibility** - "Technology should create positive change in the world"

### Supporting Messages
- 21+ years of professional experience
- Proven track record with major companies
- Innovation in development processes and tools
- Commitment to team development and mentorship

## Content Themes for Different Sections

### About Section
- Focus on the "Social Alchemist" concept
- Emphasize partnership philosophy
- Include the mentorship program as proof of values
- Connect technical expertise to business outcomes

### Services Section
- Lead with business benefits
- Include process innovations (UISL, CVA)
- Show how we're different from typical software houses
- Use client success stories as examples

### Companies We've Worked With
- Position as partnerships, not just contracts
- Highlight the complexity and scale of challenges
- Include variety of industries and company sizes
- Show progression and growth in responsibility

### Testimonials
- Choose quotes that emphasize different strengths
- Include variety of roles (technical leaders, business executives, peers)
- Show impact on both technical and business outcomes
- Include international perspective

### Blog Content
- Focus on thought leadership in technical architecture
- Address real challenges senior developers and CTOs face
- Include lessons learned from complex projects
- Connect technical decisions to business strategy

## Call-to-Action Language

### Primary CTAs
- "Let's discuss your next challenge"
- "Start a partnership conversation"
- "Explore how we can help transform your project"

### Secondary CTAs
- "Learn more about our approach"
- "See how we've solved similar challenges"
- "Connect with our team"

## Contact & Communication

### Professional but Approachable
- Use "Get in Touch" rather than formal contact language
- Include multiple ways to connect
- Mention response time expectations
- Show personality while maintaining professionalism

### Email Signature Style
```
Saulo Vallory
Chief Technology Officer
Toki Labs - Software Factory
"Technology for positive transformation"
```

## Visual Content Guidelines

### Image Captions
- Focus on the impact or story, not just what's shown
- Include context about the project or achievement
- Connect to business outcomes when relevant

### Code Examples
- Always explain the business value
- Include comments that explain the "why" not just the "what"
- Show before/after when demonstrating improvements

This style guide ensures all Toki Labs content maintains consistency while reflecting our core values of partnership, innovation, and positive impact.
